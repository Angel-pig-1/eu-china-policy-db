Proposal for a regulation on safety, resilience and sustainability of space activities in the Union

COM(2025) 335 of 25.06.2025

The Commission adopted its proposal for a Regulation on the safety, resilience and sustainability of space activities in the Union on 25 June 2025. This followed the Competitiveness Compass Communication in January 2025, which announced measures to foster a globally competitive and resilient space sector, including on a single market for space services and technologies. In addition, the mission letter to Andrius Kubilius, Commissioner designate to Defence and Space (2024), the report by Mario Draghi on the future of European competitiveness, the state of the Union letter of intent of September 2023, the Commission work programme for 2025, and the joint communication on a European Union space strategy for security and defence (March 2023), and the joint communication on an European Union approach to space traffic management (2023) underlined the need to ensure the resilience and competitiveness of the EU space sector.

The aim of the proposal is to: enhance safety, resilience and environmental sustainability of space activities; boost the internal market for space activities, space-based data and associated services, services essential for the protection of vital interests of the Union and its Member States; and to foster the global competitiveness of the EU space sector.

The proposal includes provisions on:

  • Title I: The subject matter, scope and definitions;
  • Title II: The authorisation and registration for space activities entails specific schemes for the Union space operators, the Union space operators operating Union owned assets (URSO), and the space services providers from Third Countries and international organisations.
  • Title III sets out a governance framework, which includes the appointment by each Member State of an authority in charge of authorising and supervising space activities.
  • Title IV of the proposal on technical requirements lays down the rules on safety, sustainability and resilience.

In the Parliament, the file has been referred to the ITRE committee, which has appointed its rapporteur, E. Donazzan (ECR, IT).

理事会侧进程

2025年12月9日竞争力理事会: ministers broadly endorsed the proposal's objectives while calling for greater clarification and simplification.

2025年12月5日丹麦主席国妥协文本 (Council doc. 16,437/25)

2026年3月30日塞浦路斯主席国妥协文本 — with all Member States maintaining scrutiny reservations.

2026年5月8日理事会进度报告 (Council doc. 8,861/26): 塞浦路斯主席国任内空间工作组开会九次（含与网络安全横向工作组联会一次）。未决问题：法规范围与两用活动豁免、治理架构与权责划分、避免在各国程序之上叠加平行的欧盟监管层、第三国经营者的等同机制处理。

2026年5月29日竞争力理事会: 部长们听取了进度报告通报；并就"太空促进经济安全"举行政策辩论（讨论如何在保持开放与创新的同时整合太空政策的民用、商业与安全维度）。爱尔兰代表团介绍其主席国（2026年7月起）太空政策优先事项；塞浦路斯主席国通报欧洲竞争力基金（ECF）太空部分谈判进展及《欧盟航天服务局条例》提案（COM(2026)152）进展；法国通报2026年9月9-10日巴黎大皇宫国际太空峰会。

议会侧进程

2026年3月3日: 报告员 Elena Donazzan (ECR, IT) 发布草案报告 (PE784.432)，方向是简化：授权互认（Art. 6(3)）、审查时限从12个月砍到6个月、15日内告知申请完整性、轻量制度扩展至中型企业和小型中盘股；可持续性方面保留目标与技术描述但删除"整个生命周期视觉星等至少7等"的硬性义务。

ENVI意见（2026年3月19日通过）: 要求强制环境影响评估；新增"全生命周期责任与责任承担"条款（在现有国际法义务之上叠加）。

IMCO意见（2026年4月21日通过）: 提议对欧盟立法做"太空检查"（space check）、外国投资监督。

2026年4月14日: 九个委员会修正案集提出。

后续节点: 爱尔兰主席国（2026年7月起）接手理事会侧；三读（trilogue）需理事会共同立场与议会立场齐备后方可启动。若议会中期目标（2026年中通过报告）达成，秋季进入trilogue窗口。
