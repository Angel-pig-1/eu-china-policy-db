Brussels, 7.4.2026, COM(2026) 152 final, 2026/0084(COD)

Proposal for a REGULATION OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL on the European Union Space Services Agency and amending Regulation (EU) 2021/696

(Text with EEA relevance)

EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM

1. CONTEXT OF THE PROPOSAL

Reasons for and objectives of the proposal

The founding act of the European Union Agency for the Space Programme (EUSPA) is Regulation (EU) 2021/696. Title IX provides for aims, statute, tasks and responsibilities requiring the Agency to contribute to implement the space programme components established for the period 2021-2027. While the programme was established for a period of seven years to align its duration with that of the multiannual financial framework (MFF), the Agency was not subject to that time [limitation].

In addition to its tasks stemming from Regulation (EU) 2021/696, the Agency has also been assigned responsibilities under Regulation (EU) 2023/588 establishing the Union Secure Connectivity Programme. Furthermore, upcoming initiatives, such as a proposal for a Regulation on establishing the European Competitiveness Fund ('ECF Regulation') envisage conferring more tasks to the [Agency].

The Agency's mission in support of the implementation of Union space programmes' components is now consolidated but the legal basis is embedded in a Regulation that will be partly repealed at the end of the MFF. A self-standing founding act, building on the current Regulations, and outlining clear tasks and operation rules will enhance legal certainty and ensure the Agency's continued operations and activities beyond MFF [cycles].

Consistency with existing policy provisions in the policy area

This proposal is fully consistent with the proposal for the ECF Regulation and in particular with the provisions of Section 3 (Space systems and space policy implementation) of Chapter VII (Support for resilience and security, defence industry and space), the dedicated policy window within the ECF Regulation. Once adopted, this Regulation will become the founding act of the European Union Space Services Agency which will contribute to implement the activities supported under the proposal for the ECF [Regulation].

2. LEGAL BASIS, SUBSIDIARITY AND PROPORTIONALITY

Legal basis: The proposal is based on Article 189(2) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. Regulation (EU) 2021/696 was adopted using the same legal [basis].

Subsidiarity (for non-exclusive competence): The objectives of the proposal cannot be sufficiently achieved by Member States at national, regional, or local level and are better accomplished at Union level. The proposed Regulation aims to enhance legal certainty and ensure the continuity of operations of the Agency enabling it to plan and operate effectively over the long term and beyond the MFF periods. The Agency's objective is to ensure a high degree of security for Union space activities, support the implementation of Union space components, and promote the user and market uptake of information, services, and data provided by those components – all those objectives that cannot be achieved by the Member States [individually].

Proportionality: Union action does not intend to replace national actions or authorities, nor to question their relevance.

Choice of the instrument: a Regulation is the most appropriate [instrument].

3. RESULTS OF EX-POST EVALUATIONS, STAKEHOLDER CONSULTATIONS AND IMPACT ASSESSMENTS

  • A mid-term evaluation of the Union space programme and of the performance of the Agency was carried out between Q3 2023 and Q2 2024, with a report adopted on 10 July 2024.
  • No fully-fledged impact assessment has been carried [out]（因ECF条例已做过全面影响评估且本提案基本复制现有法律基础——better regulation豁免）。

4. BUDGETARY IMPLICATIONS

The financial envelope for the Union contribution to the Agency under the MFF 2021-2027 package is EUR 525.7 million. To ensure the continuation of current tasks and to fund new activities under the Agency's mandate, this proposal provides for an increase in the contribution to the Agency for the next MFF 2028-2034, with an allocation of EUR 979.6 million.

5. OTHER ELEMENTS

  • The Agency shall submit to the Commission a report on the operation of this Regulation every five years.
  • Article 42: transitional arrangements; Article 43: deletes the specific provisions referring to the Agency in Regulation (EU) 2021/696; Article 44: entry into force, date of application 1 January [2028].

CHAPTER I PRINCIPLES: Article 1 establishes that this Regulation lays down the tasks and rules of operation of the Agency that changes its name to become the European Union Space Services Agency. Article 3: seat in Prague; possibility of local offices. Article 4 lists the Agency's own tasks, the tasks that the Commission shall entrust and those that shall be entrusted subject to operational readiness.

Agency's own tasks (Art. 4(1)): (a) security accreditation of Union space components via the Security Accreditation Board; (b) operational security of PNT and EO components; (c) operation of the space security monitoring structure; (d) user community network for government-authorised users; (e) user uptake/market development activities; (f) technical expertise to the Commission; (g) PRS access rules tasks.

Commission-entrusted tasks (Art. 4(2)): (a) managing exploitation of PNT (Galileo/EGNOS); (b) operational management of GOVSATCOM Hub; (c) downstream applications development.

Conditional tasks subject to operational readiness (Art. 4(3)): EOGS security support; GOVSATCOM and IRIS2 services provision; Space Weather Events services; SST services; radio-frequency interference monitoring; Access to space component support; Space commercialisation support (incl. CASSINI); Technological sovereignty/R&I support; cooperation with Commission to support space operators.

CHAPTER II ORGANISATION: Administrative Board, Executive Director, Deputy Executive Director (new position), Security Accreditation Board. New: Administrative Board may declare a crisis situation. SAB can give one single approval for constellations; Commission may request a decision within 3 months (deemed affirmative otherwise); Commission favourable vote required for financial/human resources matters.

CHAPTER III-VI: budget (fees for services as new revenue), staff (crisis continuity obligations, Member State secondments up to 2 years), general provisions, transitional/final provisions.

条例正文（含序言）

(以下为提案条例正文部分，含全部序言条款及第1-4条条文)

THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 189(2) thereof, ... Acting in accordance with the ordinary legislative procedure, ... HAVE ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

序言要点（逐条摘录）：
1) EUSPA由Regulation (EU) 2021/696设立，取代原GNSS Agency；行政与管理结构由Administrative Board、Executive Director、Deputy Executive Director和Security Accreditation Board组成。
2) Agency负责所有联盟太空行动的安全认证任务；基于Galileo/EGNOS推广经验承担用户采纳与下游应用开发。
3) 依Regulation (EU) 2023/588承担Union Secure Connectivity Programme职责。
4) Union Space Programme虽按MFF 2021-2027七年期设立，但Agency自身任务不受该时限限制；然而2021/696条例已不能为未来任务与运行规则提供充分框架。
5) 为确保MFF周期之外的运行连续性，应规定Agency的任务与组织方面，并更名为European Union Space Services Agency。
6) 座址Prague（Decision 2010/803/EU）；可为实施任务在成员国设local offices、在地面基础设施中心派驻人员，但不转移核心活动。
7)-20) （任务、安全认证集体责任、SAB独立性、PNT/GOVSATCOM运营经验、政府授权用户协调、市场采纳、商业化和太空经济、ENISA网络安全合作、资源、独立性、委员会赞成票、24/7战备、过渡条款等）
21) 目标不能由成员国充分实现……符合辅助性与比例性原则。

第1条 主题：本条例规定欧洲联盟航天服务局（EUSSA）的任务和运行规则。
第2条 法律地位：联盟机构，享有最广泛法律能力。
第3条 座址与地方办公室：Prague；可按程序设local offices。
第4条 任务：
1. Agency自身任务：(a) 通过SAB对联盟太空组成部分进行安全认证；(b) 获授PNT或EO组成部分开发/运行管理职责时执行运行安全（风险威胁分析、认证文件、运行安全监控）；(c) 运行太空安全监控结构（依ECF条例Art.77(3)一般安全要求及CFSP Decision 2021/698修正案Art.6指令的操作程序）；(d) 建立并管理政府授权用户社区网络；(e) 用户采纳/市场开发/宣传推广；(f) 向委员会提供技术专长；(g) 执行Galileo PRS接入规则任务（Decision 1104/2011/EU）。
2. 委员会应授予的任务：(a) PNT开发管理（含Galileo/EGNOS基础设施运维、演进、认证标准化支持、连续数据服务、EOG DPO任务、射频干扰监测与互补PNT）；(b) GOVSATCOM Hub运行管理（含运行安全）；(c) 下游、综合应用、数据生态、接收机终端开发活动。
3. 视运行准备度授予的任务：(a) EOGS与Copernicus安全支持；(b) EOGS合同管理；(c) GOVSATCOM与IRIS2政府服务提供；(d) Secure Connectivity组成部分合同管理；(e) Secure Connectivity用户方面统筹协调；(f) 用户终端支持；(g) Space Weather Events终端用户服务；(h) SST服务（政府授权用户服务除外）；(i) SST伙伴关系协议管理；(j) 射频干扰监测服务；(k) Access to space组成部分支持；(l) Space commercialisation and space economy支持（含CASSINI）；(m) Technological sovereignty, research and innovation支持；(n) 与委员会合作支持太空运营商。
