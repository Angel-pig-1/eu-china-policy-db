A. Need for an EU Space Services Agency (EUSSA)

（前言部分：欧经社认为设立EUSSA具有必要性，EUSPA改组为航天服务局是欧盟太空政策架构的重要演进……）

General comments

1.1. The EESC supports the proposal to establish a European Union Space Services Agency (EUSSA) as a successor to the European Union Agency for the Space Programme (EUSPA), with a strengthened mandate to ensure the operational delivery and security of the Union's space services and infrastructure.

1.2. The EESC considers that the creation of the EUSSA must be accompanied by a clear definition of the respective responsibilities of the Commission, the EUSSA, the European Space Agency (ESA) and the Member States, in order to avoid overlaps, duplications and gaps in the management of the Union's space activities.

1.3. The EESC underlines that the EUSSA must operate in close synergy with the European Space Agency, avoiding any duplication of functions and ensuring complementarity between operational programmes and scientific and technological activities.

1.4. The EESC stresses the importance of ensuring that the EUSSA is provided with adequate human, financial and technological resources to carry out its tasks effectively, and that its governance structure is transparent, accountable and inclusive, with the involvement of all relevant institutional, industrial and scientific stakeholders.

1.5. The EESC also highlights the importance of ensuring that the EUSSA promotes innovation, competitiveness and technological sovereignty of the European space sector, in particular by supporting small and medium‑sized enterprises (SMEs), start‑ups and emerging industrial actors.

1.6. The EESC stresses that the EUSSA must ensure a high level of security and resilience of the Union's space infrastructure and services, in cooperation with the relevant Union and national authorities, and that it must play an active role in promoting European standards and best practices at international level.

1.7. The EESC points out that the proposal should include clear provisions on the management and use of the Union's space data and services, ensuring their accessibility and interoperability for the benefit of citizens, businesses and public administrations.

1.8. The EESC lastly underlines the need for a gradual and proportionate approach to the implementation of the new framework, with a sufficient transitional period and with specific support measures for the actors involved.
