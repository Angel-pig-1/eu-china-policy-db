Xi, European Council president pledge to strengthen strategic communication, enhance cooperation, mutual trust

BEIJING, Oct. 10 (Xinhua) — Chinese President Xi Jinping met with Antonio Costa, president of the European Council, in Beijing on Friday.

Xi said that over the past five decades since the establishment of diplomatic ties between China and the European Union (EU), the two sides have always respected each other, treated each other as equals, engaged in dialogue and cooperation, and achieved mutual benefit and win-win outcomes. The bilateral relations have made positive contributions to maintaining world peace and promoting global development.

Currently, the international situation is undergoing profound and complex changes, with rising protectionism and unilateralism, Xi said, noting that the world is standing at a critical crossroads of openness versus isolation, cooperation versus confrontation.

Facing profound changes in the international landscape, China and the EU, as two major forces, two large markets, and two great civilizations, should, in the spirit of mutual respect, seeking common ground while reserving differences, open cooperation and mutual benefit, further consolidate political mutual trust, strengthen strategic communication, enhance cooperation in various fields, safeguard the authority of the multilateral trading system, and promote the building of an open world economy, Xi added.

Xi emphasized that this year marks the start of the implementation of the outcomes of the 25th China-EU leaders' meeting. China is willing to work with the EU to implement the leaders' consensus, maintain the momentum of high-level exchanges, and continuously enrich the connotation of the comprehensive strategic partnership.

Xi said that the two sides should give full play to the coordinating role of the China-EU High-level Economic and Trade Dialogue, better leverage the role of the China-EU Trade and Investment Consultation Mechanism, and promote the implementation of cooperation outcomes in the economic and trade sector, in an effort to continuously improve China-EU economic and trade relations.

Xi stressed that China and the EU should foster a more fair, transparent, non-discriminatory and predictable business environment for enterprises of both sides, jointly oppose economic decoupling, and jointly resist unilateral bullying practices, adding that China stands ready to work with the EU to inject more certainty and positive energy into a world fraught with instability and uncertainty.

Noting that multilateralism is the cornerstone of the post-war international order, Xi said that China is ready to work with the EU to practice true multilateralism, jointly uphold the authority of the United Nations, jointly defend international fairness and justice, and jointly build a community with a shared future for mankind.

Costa said that the EU-China relationship is one of the most important bilateral relationships in the world, and the EU side attaches great importance to its relations with China.

Noting that the EU-China economic and trade cooperation is mutually beneficial and win-win in nature, Costa said that this week, the two sides held the second meeting of the EU-China Trade and Investment Consultation Mechanism in Beijing and reached 16 outcomes, which is beneficial to both sides. The EU side is willing to work with China to maintain close high-level exchanges, strengthen communication and dialogue, enhance mutual trust, and promote greater development of EU-China relations.

Costa said that the EU side appreciates China's commitment to high-level opening-up, adding that the EU is ready to make joint efforts with China to safeguard multilateralism, the multilateral trading system, and a fair and reasonable international order, and jointly promote world peace, development and prosperity.

Second Meeting of China-EU Trade and Investment Consultation Mechanism Held in Beijing

BEIJING, Oct. 10 (Xinhua) — The second meeting of the China-EU Trade and Investment Consultation Mechanism was held in Beijing from Oct. 8 to 9.

The meeting was co-chaired by Li Chenggang, China's international trade representative and vice minister of commerce, and Maroš Šefčovič, European commissioner for trade and economic security.

Since the first meeting of the mechanism, positive progress has been made in China-EU trade and economic cooperation, with a series of cooperation outcomes continuously being implemented, according to a joint statement released after the meeting.

Both sides reviewed the implementation of the cooperation outcomes of the 25th China-EU leaders' meeting, and the economic and trade outcomes of President Xi Jinping's meeting with European Council President Antonio Costa and European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen, and held in-depth exchanges on key issues of mutual concern.

The two sides reached 16 outcomes in a number of areas, including:

  • the implementation of cooperation outcomes of the China-EU leaders' meeting,
  • trade in goods,
  • trade remedy cooperation,
  • food and agricultural products,
  • foreign investment access,
  • cooperation in emerging industries,
  • exchanges between the financial regulatory authorities,
  • cooperation on export controls,
  • cooperation in the pharmaceutical sector,
  • exchanges between the competent authorities in the competition field,
  • cooperation on cosmetics,
  • cooperation on standards and technical regulations,
  • cooperation on certifications, and
  • exchanges between China and the EU on World Trade Organization issues.

The two sides agreed that the European Commission's Directorate-General for Trade will maintain communication with China's Ministry of Commerce, and will strive to hold the third meeting of the China-EU Trade and Investment Consultation Mechanism in Brussels in March 2027.

During the meeting, the two sides also had in-depth exchanges on other issues of mutual concern, including economic and trade relations between China, the EU and the United States.

补充报道（同日，Von der Leyen 表态）

Also on Friday, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said that the EU and China had a "constructive meeting" on trade this week.

"We remain committed to a rebalanced trade relationship with reciprocity at its core," she said in a statement, adding that the 16 outcomes agreed at the second EU-China Trade and Investment Consultation Meeting are an important step in the right direction.
